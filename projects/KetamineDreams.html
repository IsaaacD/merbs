Ketamine Dreams

A browser-based psychedelic journey through procedurally generated dreamscapes.

The Idea

Ketamine Dreams began as an experiment in procedural visuals and evolved into something more personal. Using Three.js, GLSL shaders, and a touch of daily-seeded randomness, the project attempts to translate the sensation of a dissociative state into something viewable through any modern browser. No downloads, no installations — just open the link and drift.

The inspiration comes from real experiences. Each scene maps to a distinct phase or feeling, from the infinite corridors of ego dissolution to the claustrophobic comfort of a small enclosed space. The music track by Ellie Goulding was chosen to anchor the atmosphere.

How It Works

Under the hood, Ketamine Dreams is a real-time WebGL application. Every frame is generated client-side using a deterministic seed tied to the current date. This means anyone who joins on the same day sees the same environment, even if movement and camera control remain individual.

The post-processing stack layers bloom, edge detection, chromatic aberration, scanlines, and melt transitions to produce the signature warped, dreamlike aesthetic. A lightweight UI lets viewers tweak parameters on the fly.

Built with peer-to-peer multi-user support, the experience doubles as a shared visual canvas — think of it as a hallucination you can project for a venue, a DJ set, or a group viewing.

The Four Worlds

// nexus

The Nexus

The transitional space between scenes. An endless grid of shifting walls that feels both vast and constrained. Ego fades, patterns emerge, and the geometry itself seems alive. Wall materials morph through GLSL shaders, their hue dictated by the day's seed.

Instanced wall primitives + dynamic GLSL material morphing + bloom-driven emissive channels + edge-contrast post-processing
// space

Sparse Space

The void at the peak of the experience. Minimal geometry scattered across a massive coordinate field. Visual density comes almost entirely from the post-processing pipeline — chromatic aberration and scanlines fill the emptiness with texture. Camera speed is uncapped here, inviting high-velocity traversal through the cosmos.

Sparse volumetric primitives + chromatic aberration pass + scanline overlay + uncapped camera velocity
// lumber

Lumber

Chaos takes over. A dense forest of columnar shapes with bark-like materials that breathe and warp. The more you move, the more the scene distorts. Custom shaders drive organic surface variation, and a secondary displacement map creates a pulsing effect across every vertical surface.

Columnar geometry forest + custom organic shader + fold-intensity warping + vein-speed displacement map
// liminal

Liminal

The cube. A small, familiar-yet-uncanny room where movement is restricted and the bloom softens to a haze. This is the quietest scene, designed for reflection. Melt transitions are most prominent here, blurring the line between one dream state and the next.

Low-frequency geometry + muted color channels + reduced bloom radius + enhanced melt transitions

Tech Stack

  • Three.js — WebGL 3D rendering
  • GLSL Shaders — real-time material morphing
  • Post-processing pipeline — bloom, edge detection, chromatic aberration, scanlines
  • Daily-seeded determinism — shared experience across users
  • Peer-to-peer multi-user support with WebRTC and Peer.js
